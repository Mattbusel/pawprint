# App Review notes

---

No account, login or network connection is required.

HOW TO USE: The Today tab lists every meal and medicine due today for all pets, in time order. Tap the round button on a row to mark it given; the app records who gave it (chosen with "ticking as" at the top) and when. Tapping a dose that was given in the last few hours shows a double-dose warning. The plus button adds a dose, weight, vaccine, vet visit, note or pet. Pets shows each pet's page, Vet shows vaccines with due dates and upcoming visits, Health shows the weight chart and notes and shares a one-page vet summary PDF, and Sitter makes a printable sitter sheet PDF.

REMINDERS: turning on "Remind me" on a dose asks for notification permission (only then) and schedules local notifications at each dose time. Each notification has "Given" and "Snooze 30 min" actions. All notifications are local; nothing is sent to a server.

PRIVACY: no data is collected. Everything is stored in the app's Documents folder on the device (a JSON file and optional JPEG pet photos chosen with the system photo picker). No network requests are made.

IN-APP PURCHASE: the app is free. There is one non-consumable purchase, Pawprint Pro (com.mattbusel.pawprint.pro). Free: one pet with every feature for that pet, including all doses, reminders, vaccines, vet visits, weight and notes. Pro: adding a second and further pets, sharing the sitter sheet PDF, and sharing the vet visit summary PDF. To see the paywall: after adding a first pet, tap "Add a pet" on the Pets tab (or Pet in the plus menu); or tap "Share the sitter sheet" on the Sitter tab; or tap "Vet visit summary" on the Health tab; or tap "See Pro" on the Pawprint Pro card at the bottom of the Pets tab. "Restore purchase" is on the paywall and on that Pawprint Pro card. Reminders are never gated.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Pawprint is a pet care organizer for households where several people look after the same pets: a shared daily board of meals and medicines, dose reminders, vaccine and vet records, a weight log, and printable sheets for the vet and for a pet sitter. General audience of pet owners; rated 4+.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
No setup, login or credentials. A new install starts empty and asks for the first pet. Household members are names typed on the phone; there are no accounts and no sync between devices.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
None. No network requests, analytics, advertising or third-party frameworks. Built with SwiftUI, Swift Charts, PhotosUI (system photo picker, no photo library permission needed) and UserNotifications (local notifications only).

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. Weight can be shown in pounds or kilograms.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app gives no medical or veterinary advice; it records what the owner enters and reminds them at the times they chose. All art, text and code are my own work.
