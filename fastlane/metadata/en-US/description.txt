Did the dog get his pill? In a house where two or three people look after the pets, nobody is ever quite sure. Pawprint puts every meal and medicine for every pet on one board, reminds you on time, and remembers who gave what.

TODAY, AT A GLANCE
• Every dose and meal across all your pets, in time order
• Tap to tick. Pawprint records who gave it and when
• A warning before a double dose: "Priya gave this at 8:04 am, 2 hours ago"
• Late doses turn red so nothing slips past

REMINDERS THAT DO THE WORK
• A notification at each dose time, with Given and Snooze buttons right on it. Tick it from the lock screen without opening the app
• Daily, twice a day, every few days, weekly, or monthly flea and heartworm doses
• Pill counts that go down as you tick, with a heads-up before you run out

VET AND VACCINES
• Vaccine records with due dates worked out for you, and a strip of what is overdue or due soon
• Vet visits with a reminder the evening before
• Your vet and the 24-hour emergency vet, one tap to call

WEIGHT AND HEALTH
• A weight chart with your pet's healthy range shaded in
• A notes log for symptoms, food changes and vet visits
• A one-page vet visit summary to share: current meds, missed doses, vaccines, weight and your questions

GOING AWAY?
• A printable sitter sheet: who to call, what each pet eats, every medicine, the house notes, and a tick sheet for every day you are away

Photos of your pets, or charming drawn faces until you add one. Dogs, cats, rabbits and everyone else.

Everyone in the house ticks on the same phone, and the sitter gets the sheet. No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Pawprint is a record keeper, not medical advice. Always follow your vet's instructions.
